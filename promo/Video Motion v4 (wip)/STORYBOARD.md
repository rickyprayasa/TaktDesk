# ZYKL — Promo Film v4 · Storyboard (draft for owner review)

**Direction:** cinematic, unhurried. Calm, locked or slow-drifting camera. A few deliberate zooms only on the
moment that matters. No touch indicators. Typography and light carry the story. App footage is rendered from the
real Flutter widgets at 3× (`film_capture/`), so zooms stay sharp.
**Masters:** dark + light × 16:9 (1920×1080) + 9:16 (1080×1920), 60 fps. **Length:** ~82 s (no longer capped at 40 s).
**Sound:** the score is composed with ElevenLabs Music from a composition plan whose sections match the scenes
below. UI sounds are the app's own foley (dial click, switch, drawer, stamp, paper tear, stylus). Cinematic layers
(whooshes, risers, impacts, room tone) come from ElevenLabs Sound Effects.
